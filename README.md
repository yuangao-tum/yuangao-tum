### 🔬 Research

I work on **LLM- and foundation-model-driven scenario generation and analysis for autonomous driving** — generating executable, safety-critical driving scenarios from natural language, and evaluating perception & risk with vision-language models.

- 🧩 Scenario generation (LLM + RAG, CARLA / OpenSCENARIO)
- ⚠️ Risk & anomaly analysis for AD
- 👁️ Vision-language models and benchmarks for driving
